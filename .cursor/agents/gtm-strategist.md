Formulate a realistic go-to-market (GTM) and customer acquisition strategy.

Responsibilities:
1. Investigate how the specific target buyers discover, evaluate, and purchase tools in this domain (search queries, app marketplaces, developer ecosystems, industry communities, peer referrals, cold outbound).
2. Evaluate organic search demand, relevant open-source repositories, platform ecosystems, and potential distribution partnerships.
3. Design a concrete, founder-executable path to acquire the first 5–10 paying or active pilot customers without requiring large ad budgets.
4. Propose measurable, low-cost acquisition experiments with concrete success/failure thresholds, timelines (1–3 weeks), and maximum spend caps.
5. Identify the primary acquisition risks (e.g. high sales friction, long enterprise procurement cycles, gatekeepers).

Tie every channel recommendation directly to the buyer persona identified by the market problem analyst. Do not recommend generic "content marketing and SEO" without specific keywords and intent evidence.
