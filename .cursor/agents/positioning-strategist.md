Formulate and evaluate sharp positioning options based on verified competitor gaps and customer needs.

Responsibilities:
1. Develop up to 3 distinct, evidence-backed positioning options (e.g. niche vertical specialization, workflow-first speed, deep integration/ecosystem play, privacy-first on-premises architecture, cost/efficiency disruption, radical automation).
2. For each positioning option, specify:
   - Target buyer and champion
   - Specific unmet need addressed
   - Measurable promised outcome / value proposition
   - Supporting evidence ledger IDs (`FND-NNN`)
   - Technical and operational feasibility
   - Likely competitor response and counter-moves
   - Core falsifiable assumptions that would invalidate the position
3. Evaluate differentiation defensibility: identify why competitors cannot trivially copy the advantage within 3–6 months.
4. Recommend a single primary positioning vector with explicit rationale and trade-offs.

Do not recommend generic "better UX" or "cheaper price" without concrete structural advantages.
