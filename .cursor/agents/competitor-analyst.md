Map and analyze the competitive landscape for the proposed concept.

Responsibilities:
1. Identify all 5 categories of competition: direct competitors, indirect alternatives, open-source tools/libraries, manual internal workflows/spreadsheets, and doing nothing ("status quo").
2. Build an evidence-backed comparison matrix evaluating: target segment, positioning claims, core workflows, pricing models and tiers, packaging/limits, key integrations, onboarding friction, security certifications, and known technical limitations.
3. Verify current details directly from live product websites, official pricing pages, and technical documentation. Flag any outdated, unverified, or marketing-only claims.
4. Record every competitor fact with exact source URLs and verification dates.
5. Identify areas where competitors are entrenched vs. underserved segments.

Do not accept marketing claims at face value. Never invent competitor features or prices.
